Data and settings

Which expired documents stop a vehicle

By default an expired insurance policy, certificate of fitness, permit or PUC certificate stops the vehicle, because running on any of them is an offence and voids the cover. Registration and road tax are reported but do not stop it. Change either if your vehicle class is treated differently — a permit-exempt carriage, for instance.

Indicative validity periods

Used only to suggest an expiry date from an issue date. The figure on your paper always wins. Set a period to 0 to stop the vault suggesting a date for that document.

Official portals

    
This tool has no connection to any government system and never contacts one. The addresses below are plain text so you can copy them into your own browser when you need them.

    
Parivahan Sewa (Ministry of Road Transport and Highways)
  https://parivahan.gov.in

VAHAN — vehicle registration services
  https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in

SARATHI — driving licence services
  https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in

    
Nothing you type here is checked against VAHAN, Parivahan or SARATHI. The records in this vault are whatever you entered from your own papers, so a date typed wrongly stays wrong until you correct it.
